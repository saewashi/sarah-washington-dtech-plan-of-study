All 22 required core credits (TCM 55000 set as the core elective; swap for ENGT 60500 if preferred) and the 15 dissertation credits are already scheduled in prerequisite order. Per the official 2025-26 catalog, the program requires 53 selective credits of which 30 can come from a master's degree, so with the 30 transfer confirmed, exactly 23 elective credits remain. Spring 2028 has room for 3, then Fall 2028, Spring 2029, and Fall 2029 each take about 6; since 23 is not divisible by 3, one or two 1-credit CNIT courses, or one heavier semester, lands it exactly. The catalog approves selectives from AT, CNIT, CEMT, OLS, and ECET (50000 to 69999 level) with Chair approval, plus the listed TECH doctorate electives. Per the program webinar, these 23 credits may be filled with an interdisciplinary mix or by a concentration or graduate certificate (for example the Construction Management concentration), whose courses count toward the elective hours.

Working Direction

Trust calibration and risk perception in AI-mediated information: how organizations and vulnerable populations (including children and teens growing up with these technologies) evaluate, trust, and act on AI-generated content, with applied outcomes for human-centered cybersecurity design. Builds on prior capstone research evaluating five AI tools across 11 legal practice areas.
